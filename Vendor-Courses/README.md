# Vendor courses

These are the online courses you do between sessions. Each course has its own folder with one note inside, which says the provider, the dates to do it between, and which session it is due before.

**This folder is read-only.** It is part of your course files, which are updated before each session, so anything you type in here would be overwritten. To keep your own notes and proof, copy the courses into your own vault (the `my-learning-vault` folder) and work there.

## Copy it into your own vault

1. In Obsidian, open `Programme` in your own vault. Make a folder inside it called `Vendor-Courses`.
2. Copy the course folders from `Course-Files`, then `Vendor-Courses`, into that new folder. Your AI can do this for you: ask it to "copy every course folder from Course-Files/Vendor-Courses into Programme/Vendor-Courses, and don't change the originals".
3. Work from your own copy. Do not edit the files in `Course-Files`.

## Find a course

Go to the provider's website and search for the course name. Course links change often, so each note gives the website, not a direct link.

## When you finish a course

In **your own copy** of the course note:

1. Under **Proof I finished**, add your certificate, badge link or a screenshot.
2. At the top of the note, change `status: not started` to `status: finished`.

Your own list may have a few more courses than the ones here. Tony will give you the list, and your AI can add them to your own copy.
